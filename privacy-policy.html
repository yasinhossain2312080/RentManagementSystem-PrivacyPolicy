Privacy Policy

Rent Management System

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Rent Management System may store information entered by the user while using the application. This may include tenant names, tenant identification numbers, addresses, phone numbers, rental information, rent payments, property information, and account information.

2. Google Account

The application allows users to connect a Google Account to use Google Drive for backup and restore purposes.

The application does not collect or store the user's Google password. Google authentication is handled through Google's authentication system.

3. Google Drive Data

If the user enables Google Drive backup, application backup files may be uploaded to the user's Google Drive account.

Users can disconnect their Google Account or revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.

4. How We Use Information

Information stored in the application is used only to provide the application's features, including tenant management, rent payment management, property information management, account management, and backup and restore functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or share users' information for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

Application data may be stored locally on the user's computer. If Google Drive backup is enabled, backup files may also be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their computer, application access, and Google Account.

6. Data Deletion

Users may delete application data stored on their computer. Users may also delete backup files stored in their Google Drive.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google services, including Google Drive and Google authentication. The use of these services is subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
