Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Rent Management System. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user information and data.

1. Information We Collect

Rent Management System may store information entered by the user, including tenant information, rental payments, property information, account information, and other data required to operate the application.

2. Google Account

The application allows users to connect a Google account for Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

When a user connects a Google account, the application uses Google's authentication system to authorize access. The application does not collect or store the user's Google account password.

3. Google Drive Data

The application may use Google Drive to store backup files created by the user. These backup files are used only for backup and restore functionality within the application.

Users can disconnect their Google account or revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.

4. How We Use Information

Information stored by the application is used to provide features such as tenant management, rent payment management, property management, account management, backup, and restore.

We do not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

The application stores its data locally on the user's computer. When the user enables Google Drive backup, backup files may also be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their computer, application account, and Google account.

6. Data Deletion

Users may delete application data from their computer. Google Drive backup files can also be deleted by the user from their Google Drive account.

7. Third-Party Services

Rent Management System uses Google services for Google account authentication and Google Drive backup functionality. Google's services are subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
